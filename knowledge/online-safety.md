# Staying safe online · សុវត្ថិភាពតាមអ៊ីនធឺណិត

- Use a different, long password for each important account (email, bank, Facebook, Telegram), or a password manager.
  A long phrase of several words is stronger than a short word with symbols.
- Turn on two-step verification (ការផ្ទៀងផ្ទាត់ពីរជំហាន / 2FA) for email, Facebook and Telegram. In Telegram:
  Settings → Privacy and Security → Two-Step Verification.
- Never share a one-time code (OTP / លេខកូដ) sent by SMS, a bank PIN or a password with anyone — not even someone who
  says they are from the bank, the police, Telegram or Facebook. Real staff never ask for them.
- Keep the phone and apps updated, and install apps only from the Play Store or App Store.

# Common scams · ការបោកប្រាស់ដែលកើតមានញឹកញាប់

- "You won a prize" messages that ask for a fee, your card details or a code.
- A friend's account that suddenly asks to borrow money: call the friend to check first; the account may be hacked.
- Fake bank, tax or police calls that threaten you and ask you to transfer money or install an app.
- Job offers abroad with very high pay, free travel and no interview: these can lead to forced work in scam compounds.
  Check the company, never hand over your passport, and tell your family where you are going.
- Online shops that only accept payment in advance and disappear; links that look like a bank's site but are not.
- Investment or crypto schemes that promise high, guaranteed profit.

If you were scammed: stop paying, change your passwords, call your bank at once using the number on its official
website or card, keep screenshots, and report to the police (117).

# Social media · បណ្ដាញសង្គម

- Think before posting: photos can show where you live or study. Do not post ID cards, tickets or bank QR codes.
- Check news before sharing: look for the same story on trusted news sites, check the date, and be careful with
  shocking headlines and edited photos.
- Cyberbullying (ការសម្លុតតាមអ៊ីនធឺណិត): do not reply, keep screenshots, block and report the account, and tell a
  trusted adult or teacher.
