# Khmer grammar basics · វេយ្យាករណ៍ខ្មែរ

- Word order is subject–verb–object: ខ្ញុំ (I) ញ៉ាំ (eat) បាយ (rice) = I eat rice.
- Verbs never change form. Time is shown with words, not endings: បាន (past, completed): ខ្ញុំបានទៅ "I went"; នឹង (future): ខ្ញុំនឹងទៅ "I will go"; កំពុង (in progress): ខ្ញុំកំពុងរៀន "I am studying"; ធ្លាប់ (ever, used to): ខ្ញុំធ្លាប់ទៅសៀមរាប "I have been to Siem Reap"; ហើយ at the end (already): ខ្ញុំញ៉ាំហើយ "I have eaten".
- Time words such as ម្សិលមិញ (yesterday), ថ្ងៃនេះ (today), ថ្ងៃស្អែក (tomorrow) can go at the start or the end of the sentence. Both «ម្សិលមិញ ខ្ញុំទៅសាលារៀន» and «ខ្ញុំទៅសាលារៀនម្សិលមិញ» are correct.
- Adjectives come after the noun: ផ្ទះធំ (a big house), សៀវភៅថ្មី (a new book). Very: ណាស់ after the adjective (ល្អណាស់) or ខ្លាំង.
- Questions: add ទេ or ឬទេ at the end for yes/no (អ្នកសុខសប្បាយទេ?); question words: អ្វី (what), នរណា / អ្នកណា (who), ណា (which), ពេលណា (when), នៅឯណា (where), ហេតុអ្វី (why), យ៉ាងដូចម្តេច (how), ប៉ុន្មាន (how many / how much). តើ at the start marks a question in formal or written Khmer.
- Negation: មិន ... ទេ around the verb: ខ្ញុំមិនដឹងទេ "I don't know". Don't (command): កុំ: កុំភ្លេច "Don't forget".
- Plural is usually not marked; use numbers, ពួក (ពួកគេ "they"), or doubling with ៗ (ក្មេងៗ "children").

# Classifiers · ពាក្យសម្រាប់រាប់ (ចំនួន + ពាក្យរាប់)

The pattern is noun + number + classifier: សិស្សបីនាក់ (three students).
- នាក់ for people · ក្បាល for animals and books (សៀវភៅពីរក្បាល) · គ្រឿង for machines and vehicles (ឡានមួយគ្រឿង) · ដើម for trees, pens and long things · ខ្នង for houses · ផ្លែ for fruit · គ្រាប់ for small round things (eggs, seeds, bullets) · សន្លឹក for sheets of paper · ចាន for plates of food · កែវ for glasses of drink · អង្គ for monks and royalty.

# Politeness and pronouns · ការគោរព និងសព្វនាម

- Greeting: ជម្រាបសួរ (formal, with hands joined: សំពះ), សួស្តី (everyday). Goodbye: ជម្រាបលា (formal), លាហើយ (casual).
- Yes: បាទ (said by men), ចាស (said by women). Thank you: អរគុណ / សូមអរគុណ. Sorry / excuse me: សុំទោស.
- I: ខ្ញុំ (polite, everyday). You: អ្នក (neutral), លោក (to a man, polite), លោកស្រី (to a woman, polite), បង (older sibling, also used for someone a little older), ប្អូន (younger person). He/she: គាត់ (polite) or គេ. We: យើង. They: ពួកគេ.
- Teachers are addressed as លោកគ្រូ (male) and អ្នកគ្រូ (female). Monks are addressed as ព្រះតេជព្រះគុណ.
- Royal and religious language uses special words (រាជសព្ទ): for the King, eat is សោយ and sleep is ផ្ទំ.

# Writing system · អក្សរខ្មែរ

- Khmer script has 33 consonants (ព្យញ្ជនៈ ៣៣ តួ). Consonants are in two series: the first series (អក្សរសំឡេងអ) and the second series (អក្សរសំឡេងអូ); the same vowel sign sounds different after each series.
- A consonant written under another is a subscript (ជើង), for example the ្រ in ស្រី.
- Khmer does not put spaces between words. A space separates phrases or clauses, and ។ (ខណ្ឌ) ends a sentence. ៕ ends a whole text or story. ៗ repeats the word before it (ផ្សេងៗ). Quotations use « ».
- Khmer is written left to right. The Khmer script comes from the Pallava script of southern India.

# Commonly confused words · ពាក្យដែលច្រឡំញឹកញាប់

- ពន្លឺ (light) is not ពន្ធ (tax).
- ខែឧសភា is May; ខែមេសា is April.
- ទៅ (go) and ទូ (cupboard); នៅ (at, still, to live) and នូវ (object marker in formal writing).
- បាន after a verb means "able to" (ខ្ញុំទៅបាន "I can go"); before a verb it marks the past (ខ្ញុំបានទៅ "I went").

# Word meanings · អត្ថន័យពាក្យ

- សប្បុរសធម៌ (charity): kind, generous acts that help other people, especially those in need, without expecting anything back; for example giving food, money or time to the poor. អង្គការសប្បុរសធម៌ is a charity organisation. Example: «ក្រុមនិស្សិតបានធ្វើសប្បុរសធម៌ ដោយចែកអង្ករដល់គ្រួសារក្រីក្រ។»
- សាមគ្គីភាព (solidarity, unity) · វប្បធម៌ (culture) · អរិយធម៌ (civilisation) · បរិស្ថាន (environment) · សេដ្ឋកិច្ច (economy) · បច្ចេកវិទ្យា (technology) · អប់រំ (education) · សុខភាព (health).

# Proverbs · សុភាសិតខ្មែរ

- «ចេះពីរៀន មានពីរក» — Knowledge comes from studying; wealth comes from working for it. Meaning: success needs effort.
- «ទន់ពេកគេច្របាច់ រឹងពេកគេកាច់» — Too soft and people squeeze you; too hard and people break you. Meaning: be balanced, neither too weak nor too stubborn.
- «ទឹកឡើង ត្រីស៊ីស្រមោច ទឹកស្រក ស្រមោចស៊ីត្រី» — Fortunes change, so do not mistreat the weak (see cambodia-facts.md).
