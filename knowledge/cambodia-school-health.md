# School system and the BacII exam · ប្រព័ន្ធអប់រំ និងការប្រឡងបាក់ឌុប

- Cambodian general education is 12 years: primary school grades 1–6 (បឋមសិក្សា), lower secondary grades 7–9 (អនុវិទ្យាល័យ), upper secondary grades 10–12 (វិទ្យាល័យ). Schools are run by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (ក្រសួងអប់រំ យុវជន និងកីឡា, MoEYS).
- There is a national exam at the end of grade 9 (សញ្ញាបត្រមធ្យមសិក្សាបឋមភូមិ) and at the end of grade 12: the BacII (បាក់ឌុប, សញ្ញាបត្រមធ្យមសិក្សាទុតិយភូមិ), needed for university.
- BacII results are graded A, B, C, D, E (pass) and F (fail). Students choose the science track (ថ្នាក់វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រ) or the social-science track (ថ្នាក់វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រសង្គម). Exam dates are announced by the ministry each year.
- Study advice for the BacII: make a weekly plan, study in short focused blocks with breaks, practise past exam papers under time limits, review mistakes, sleep enough, and ask teachers about topics you do not understand.
- Major public universities: Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP, 1960), Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC, 1964), Royal University of Law and Economics (RULE), National University of Management (NUM), University of Health Sciences (UHS), Royal University of Fine Arts (RUFA), Royal University of Agriculture (RUA).

# Health basics · សុខភាពមូលដ្ឋាន

Sulvana is not a doctor. For serious or worsening illness, see a health worker or hospital.
- Emergency numbers in Cambodia: police 117, fire 118, ambulance 119 (លេខសង្គ្រោះបន្ទាន់៖ ប៉ូលិស ១១៧, ពន្លត់អគ្គិភ័យ ១១៨, រថយន្តសង្គ្រោះ ១១៩).
- Fever (គ្រុនក្ដៅ): a temperature of 38 °C or more. Give plenty of fluids, light clothing, rest, and paracetamol at the dose on the packet for the person's age or weight. A baby under 3 months with 38 °C or more must see a doctor straight away. See a doctor urgently for fever with a stiff neck, fits, difficulty breathing, unusual sleepiness, a rash that does not fade when pressed, or fever lasting more than 2–3 days.
- Dehydration and diarrhoea: give oral rehydration solution (ORS, ទឹកអំបិលស្ករ) in small frequent sips and keep feeding. Danger signs: no urine for many hours, very dry mouth, sunken eyes, extreme tiredness.
- Dengue fever (គ្រុនឈាម) is spread by Aedes mosquitoes that bite in the daytime and breed in standing water; it is common in the rainy season. Use paracetamol, not aspirin or ibuprofen. Warning signs (go to hospital): severe stomach pain, repeated vomiting, bleeding gums or nose, black stools, restlessness or great tiredness, especially as the fever falls. Prevention: empty or cover water containers, use mosquito nets and repellent.
- Malaria (គ្រុនចាញ់) occurs mainly in forested and border areas; it is spread by night-biting mosquitoes and needs a blood test and treatment.
- Snakebite: keep the person calm and still, remove rings and tight items, and go to hospital quickly. Do not cut the wound, suck out venom or use a tight tourniquet.
- Kantha Bopha (គន្ធបុប្ផា) children's hospitals in Phnom Penh and Siem Reap treat children free of charge.
