# Timeline of Cambodian history · កាលប្បវត្តិប្រវត្តិសាស្ត្រកម្ពុជា

- Funan (ហ្វូណន): about the 1st to the 6th century, a trading kingdom in the Mekong delta.
- Chenla (ចេនឡា): about the 6th to the 8th century. Its temples at Sambor Prei Kuk (សំបូរព្រៃគុក) in Kampong Thom are a UNESCO World Heritage Site (2017).
- 802: King Jayavarman II (ព្រះបាទជ័យវរ្ម័នទី២) declared himself universal king on Phnom Kulen (ភ្នំគូលែន), the traditional start of the Angkor Empire.
- Early 12th century: Suryavarman II built Angkor Wat. Late 12th to early 13th century: Jayavarman VII built Angkor Thom and the Bayon and many hospitals and rest houses.
- 1431: the traditional date of the fall of Angkor after an attack from Ayutthaya (Siam). The capital later moved south (Longvek, Oudong, then Phnom Penh).
- 1863: Cambodia became a French protectorate (អាណាព្យាបាលបារាំង).
- 9 November 1953: independence from France under King Norodom Sihanouk (ព្រះបាទនរោត្តម សីហនុ).
- 1970: Lon Nol's coup created the Khmer Republic.
- 17 April 1975 – 7 January 1979: the Khmer Rouge (ខ្មែរក្រហម) regime of Democratic Kampuchea, under which about 1.5 to 2 million people died. Tuol Sleng (S-21) in Phnom Penh is now a genocide museum.
- 23 October 1991: the Paris Peace Agreements. 1993: elections organised by the UN (UNTAC), a new constitution and the restoration of the monarchy.
- 1999: Cambodia joined ASEAN. 2004: King Norodom Sihamoni was crowned on 29 October.
- UNESCO World Heritage Sites: Angkor (1992), the Temple of Preah Vihear (2008), Sambor Prei Kuk (2017), Koh Ker (2023).

# Geography · ភូមិសាស្ត្រ

- Area about 181,035 km². Population about 17 million (mid-2020s estimates). Capital Phnom Penh (ភ្នំពេញ). 24 provinces plus the capital.
- Neighbours: Thailand to the west and north-west, Laos to the north-east, Vietnam to the east and south-east; the Gulf of Thailand to the south-west, with a coastline of about 440 km (Koh Kong, Preah Sihanouk, Kampot, Kep).
- The Mekong (ទន្លេមេគង្គ) flows north to south through the east of the country. The Tonle Sap (ទន្លេសាប) lake is the largest freshwater lake in Southeast Asia: in the rainy season the Mekong's flood pushes water up the Tonle Sap river into the lake, which grows several times larger, and in the dry season the flow reverses and the lake drains back.
- Mountains: the Cardamom Mountains (ជួរភ្នំក្រវាញ) in the south-west, with Phnom Aural (ភ្នំឱរ៉ាល់, about 1,813 m, Kampong Speu), the highest point; the Dangrek Mountains (ជួរភ្នំដងរែក) along the northern border with Thailand; the Elephant Mountains (ជួរភ្នំដំរី) near the coast.
- The largest cities include Phnom Penh, Siem Reap, Battambang, Preah Sihanouk (Kampong Som) and Poipet.

# State and symbols · រដ្ឋ និងនិមិត្តរូបជាតិ

- Motto: ជាតិ សាសនា ព្រះមហាក្សត្រ (Nation, Religion, King). Official language: Khmer. Main religion: Theravada Buddhism.
- National anthem: នគររាជ (Nokor Reach). The flag shows Angkor Wat in white on a red band between two blue bands.
- Currency: the riel (រៀល, KHR); US dollars are also widely used.
- Parliament has two houses: the National Assembly (រដ្ឋសភា) and the Senate (ព្រឹទ្ធសភា).
