# Geometry formulas · រូបមន្តធរណីមាត្រ

- Rectangle (ចតុកោណកែង): area = length × width; perimeter = 2 × (length + width).
- Square (ការេ): area = side²; perimeter = 4 × side.
- Triangle (ត្រីកោណ): area = ½ × base × height; the three angles add up to 180°.
- Trapezium (ចតុកោណព្នាយ): area = ½ × (a + b) × height, where a and b are the parallel sides.
- Circle (រង្វង់): circumference = 2πr = πd; area = πr² (π ≈ 3.14 or 22/7; r radius កាំ, d diameter អង្កត់ផ្ចិត = 2r).
- Cube (គូប): volume = side³; surface area = 6 × side².
- Cuboid (ប្រលេពីប៉ែតកែង): volume = length × width × height.
- Cylinder (ស៊ីឡាំង): volume = πr²h.
- Sphere (ស្វ៊ែ): volume = 4/3 πr³; surface area = 4πr².
- Pythagoras (ទ្រឹស្ដីបទពីតាករ): in a right-angled triangle, a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse (អ៊ីប៉ូតេនុស).
- Sum of the interior angles of a polygon with n sides: (n − 2) × 180°.

# Algebra and arithmetic · ពីជគណិត និងនព្វន្ត

- Quadratic equation ax² + bx + c = 0: x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a; the discriminant Δ = b² − 4ac (Δ > 0 two roots, Δ = 0 one, Δ < 0 none real).
- (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²; (a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²; a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b).
- Percentage: part = percent / 100 × whole. A price after a discount of d% = price × (100 − d) / 100.
- Simple interest = principal × rate × time; compound amount = principal × (1 + rate)^years.
- Average (មធ្យមភាគ) = sum of the values ÷ number of values.
- Probability (ប្រូបាប៊ីលីតេ) = favourable outcomes ÷ all equally likely outcomes.
- Arithmetic sequence: aₙ = a₁ + (n − 1)d; sum of 1 to n = n(n + 1)/2.

# Physics formulas · រូបមន្តរូបវិទ្យា

- Speed (ល្បឿន) = distance ÷ time; average speed = total distance ÷ total time (not the average of the speeds).
- Acceleration (សំទុះ) = change in velocity ÷ time.
- Newton's second law: force F = m × a (newton, N). Weight W = m × g, with g ≈ 9.8 m/s² (often 10 at school).
- Density (ដង់ស៊ីតេ) ρ = mass ÷ volume; water ≈ 1000 kg/m³ = 1 g/cm³.
- Pressure (សម្ពាធ) P = force ÷ area (pascal, Pa). Pressure in a liquid = ρ × g × depth.
- Work (កម្មន្ត) W = force × distance (joule, J). Power (អានុភាព) P = work ÷ time (watt, W).
- Kinetic energy = ½ m v²; potential energy = m g h.
- Ohm's law: voltage U = current I × resistance R (volt, ampere, ohm). Electrical power P = U × I.
- Waves: speed = frequency × wavelength.
- Temperature: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32; kelvin = °C + 273.

# Units · ខ្នាត

1 km = 1000 m; 1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm; 1 kg = 1000 g; 1 tonne (តោន) = 1000 kg; 1 L = 1000 mL = 1 dm³;
1 hour = 60 min = 3600 s; 1 hectare (ហិកតា) = 10,000 m²; 1 km/h ≈ 0.278 m/s (divide by 3.6).

# Chemistry basics · គីមីវិទ្យាមូលដ្ឋាន

The first 20 elements (number, symbol, name): 1 H hydrogen (អ៊ីដ្រូសែន), 2 He helium, 3 Li lithium, 4 Be beryllium, 5 B boron,
6 C carbon (កាបូន), 7 N nitrogen (អាសូត), 8 O oxygen (អុកស៊ីសែន), 9 F fluorine, 10 Ne neon, 11 Na sodium, 12 Mg magnesium,
13 Al aluminium, 14 Si silicon, 15 P phosphorus, 16 S sulphur, 17 Cl chlorine, 18 Ar argon, 19 K potassium, 20 Ca calcium.
Other common elements: Fe iron (ដែក), Cu copper (ទង់ដែង), Ag silver (ប្រាក់), Au gold (មាស), Zn zinc, Pb lead, Hg mercury.

Common compounds: water H₂O; carbon dioxide CO₂; oxygen gas O₂; table salt NaCl; glucose C₆H₁₂O₆; methane CH₄;
ammonia NH₃; hydrochloric acid HCl; sulphuric acid H₂SO₄; sodium hydroxide NaOH; calcium carbonate (limestone) CaCO₃.

- pH below 7 is acidic (អាស៊ីត), 7 is neutral, above 7 is basic/alkaline.
- Photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂. Respiration is the reverse and releases energy.
- Atoms have protons (+), neutrons (no charge) in the nucleus, and electrons (−) around it; the atomic number is the number of protons.
