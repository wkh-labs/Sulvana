# Kroeung, the Khmer spice paste · គ្រឿង

Kroeung (គ្រឿង) is the pounded herb paste at the base of many Khmer dishes. Its main ingredients are lemongrass (ស្លឹកគ្រៃ), galangal (រំដេង), kaffir lime leaves and zest (ស្លឹកក្រូចសើច), turmeric (រមៀត), garlic (ខ្ទឹមស) and shallots (ខ្ទឹមក្រហម). Some recipes add fingerroot (ខ្ជាយ) or dried chilli. Yellow kroeung has more turmeric; green kroeung uses the lemongrass leaves.

# Fish amok · អាម៉ុកត្រី

Ingredients: firm freshwater fish fillet (often snakehead, ត្រីរ៉ស់), yellow kroeung, coconut milk (ខ្ទិះដូង), egg, fish sauce, palm sugar (ស្ករត្នោត), a little shrimp paste or prahok, and noni leaves (ស្លឹកញរ). Method: mix the kroeung with coconut milk, egg, fish sauce and sugar; add sliced fish; line banana-leaf cups (ស្លឹកចេក) with noni leaves; fill them, top with thick coconut cream, and steam for about 20–30 minutes until set. It is a soft, mildly spicy steamed curry, not fried and not made with cow's milk.

# Other well-known dishes · ម្ហូបល្បីៗផ្សេងទៀត

- Samlor korko (សម្លកកូរ): "stirred soup", often called the national soup: kroeung, prahok, fish or pork, many vegetables (green papaya, pumpkin, long beans, eggplant) and roasted ground rice to thicken it.
- Samlor machu (សម្លម្ជូរ): sour soup made sour with tamarind or other sour fruit, with fish or meat and vegetables.
- Kuy teav (គុយទាវ): rice-noodle soup in a clear pork broth with sliced meat, often eaten for breakfast, with lime, chilli and herbs on the side.
- Num banh chok (នំបញ្ចុក): fresh rice noodles eaten with a fish and green kroeung curry sauce (នំបញ្ចុកសម្លខ្មែរ), served with raw vegetables and herbs; a common breakfast.
- Bai sach chrouk (បាយសាច់ជ្រូក): grilled marinated pork on broken rice with pickled vegetables, a breakfast dish.
- Lok lak (ឡុកឡាក់): stir-fried marinated beef cubes served with a dip of lime juice, salt and Kampot pepper.
- Prahok (ប្រហុក): fermented fish paste, a basic Khmer ingredient used in soups and dips; prahok ktis (ប្រហុកខ្ទិះ) is a dip of prahok cooked with pork and coconut milk.
- Nom krok (នំគ្រក់): small round coconut-and-rice-flour cakes cooked in a dimpled pan.
- Kampot pepper (ម្រេចកំពត): pepper grown in Kampot and Kep, protected by a geographical indication (GI) since 2010.
