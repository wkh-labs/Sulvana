# Money and payments · លុយ និងការទូទាត់

- Cambodia's currency is the riel (៛, KHR). US dollars are widely used for larger amounts; small change is usually given in riel. The exchange rate is roughly 4,000–4,100 riel per US dollar (it changes; check the current rate).
- Bakong (បាគង) is the National Bank of Cambodia's payment system (launched in 2020). KHQR is the national QR-code standard, so one QR code can be paid from most Cambodian banking apps.
- Common banks with mobile apps include ABA, ACLEDA, Canadia, Wing and others. Never share a PIN, password or one-time code (OTP) with anyone, even someone who says they are from the bank.

# Phones and internet · ទូរស័ព្ទ និងអ៊ីនធឺណិត

- Cambodia's country calling code is +855. Mobile operators include Smart, Cellcard and Metfone. A SIM card must be registered with an ID card or passport.
- Common scams: messages claiming you won a prize, fake bank or police calls asking for money or codes, and job offers abroad that promise high pay. Do not click unknown links, and check with the bank or police directly.

# Practical facts · ព័ត៌មានប្រចាំថ្ងៃ

- Traffic drives on the right. Helmets are required on motorbikes for the rider and passenger.
- Electricity is 230 V, 50 Hz.
- Time zone UTC+7, the same as Thailand and Vietnam.
- Main airports: Phnom Penh and Siem Reap (Siem Reap–Angkor International Airport opened in 2023); Sihanoukville also has an international airport.
- The national ID card is អត្តសញ្ញាណប័ណ្ណសញ្ជាតិខ្មែរ; a birth certificate is សំបុត្រកំណើត; a family book is សៀវភៅគ្រួសារ.
